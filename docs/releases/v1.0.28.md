# PlanetCreations 1.0.28 — Stream smarter. Build together.

This release brings streaming, Communities and collaborative creation together in one much more connected PlanetCreations experience.

## 🎥 A new Stream Management experience

- The Creation picker is now the standard way to start a stream, with an optional **Experimental Auto Mode** for creators who want PlanetCreations to select the most likely Creation automatically.
- Auto Mode compares the stream title with your own Creation titles, types, games, categories and tags. Title matches are weighted strongly, while Creation type helps distinguish, for example, a park from a flat ride.
- When a manually selected Creation is locked, title changes no longer replace it silently. PlanetCreations instead suggests the new best match in a stream notification.
- Stream Management opens automatically when a stream starts and stays synchronized between linked devices, including remote-streaming setups.
- A dedicated always-on-top management window keeps the picker, stream status, notification history and notification settings close to the game without taking over the normal overlay.

## 🟣 Twitch + 🔴 YouTube, together

- One PlanetCreations live session can now represent the same stream on Twitch and YouTube simultaneously.
- Both platforms are shown on the active Creation, and ending the stream from OBS or Streamlabs closes the complete dual-stream session.
- A platform-specific offline event only removes that output when the other platform is still live.
- PlanetCreationsBot can participate in multiple creators' chats at once and supports `!Creation`, `!Builder` and `!Community` on the active platforms.
- Builder and Community information is prefetched once per stream, while the active Creation remains instantly updateable.

## 🔔 Notifications where they matter

- Stream-relevant notifications now live in Stream Management instead of remaining in the regular notification bell.
- Visible notifications can appear as a compact popover beneath the overlay icon.
- Mute controls support general notifications, stream notifications, timed muting and muting for the remainder of the stream.
- General notification preferences remain available from the bell and are mirrored in Stream Management, so settings are easy to find in either context.

## ⭐ Official Partner Communities

- The Community Hub gains a dedicated Partner Communities experience that appears automatically when official partners exist.
- Partner cards now feature a centered, information-rich layout with the latest three YouTube videos, clickable social links, supported games and direct Community navigation.
- Official partners receive a distinctive badge on their cards and Community banner, with their Community logo integrated into the badge presentation.
- Partner status can only be assigned or removed by PlanetCreations administrators, with protection enforced beyond the visible client controls.

## 🚀 Faster, scalable discovery

- Creation, homepage, Community and YouTube video indexes now use a segmented rollover design instead of relying on a single ever-growing document.
- Homepage discovery can combine Creations across all index segments, while Community video galleries load newer segments first and fetch older videos only as visitors continue browsing.
- New Communities and newly connected YouTube channels receive an automatic video backfill, while partner cards stay focused on the three most recent videos.

## 🤝 Safer collaboration publishing

- Collaboration invitations, membership changes and publishing access have been further hardened with server-authoritative checks.
- Completion, contributor consent, signed-save publication, profile credits and unanimous publication revocation now form one guarded workflow.
- Additional production smoke coverage verifies installed-client collaboration flows and protects published packages from unauthorized mutation.

## ✨ Polish and reliability

- Restored Community theme colors and refined selector and floating-button motion.
- Updated Firebase and desktop dependencies, expanded automated coverage and tightened production deployment safeguards.
- The hosted interface continues to update quickly, while this desktop release adds the native windows and IPC bridge required by the new overlay, notification and Stream Management features.

Thank you for building, sharing and streaming with PlanetCreations. 💙
