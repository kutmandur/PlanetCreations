PlanetCreations

PlanetCreations is an independent community workshop for Planet Coaster, Planet Coaster 2 and Planet Zoo. Discover and share parks, blueprints and other creations, join community events, and collaborate with other builders.

Workshop and desktop save manager

The free PlanetCreations Client combines the online workshop with local save management. Browse community creations, scan supported game files, view embedded previews and available metadata, create or restore backups, and manage matching Custom Media.

PlanetCreations is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Frontier Developments. The supported games and any required DLC must be purchased separately.
