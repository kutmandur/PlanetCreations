Community Content Guidelines

Effective date: 23 August 2026

These rules apply to creations, files, images, videos, comments, profiles, communities, events, collaborations and all other user-generated content on PlanetCreations.

Share only content you may use

Upload only content you created or have permission to publish. Respect copyrights, trademarks, privacy, publicity rights and the rules of games and third-party services. Do not claim an official relationship with Frontier Developments or another person or organization.

Keep the community safe

Do not post illegal content, malware, executable payloads, attempts to bypass package verification, scams, credential theft, doxxing, threats, harassment, hateful content, sexual exploitation, pornography or content that promotes serious harm. Content that exploits or endangers children is strictly prohibited.

Be accurate

Titles, descriptions, tags, screenshots and file contents must accurately represent the Creation. Do not impersonate others, manipulate ratings or reports, spam, or deliberately publish corrupted or misleading packages.

Reports and moderation

Use the in-product Report action when content or a user may violate these guidelines. Reports are reviewed by PlanetCreations moderators. Action may include hiding or removing content, restricting features, warnings, strikes, suspension or account termination. Limited records may be preserved for abuse prevention and appeals.

Removal requests and appeals

PlanetCreations may remove or disable content that violates these guidelines, applicable law, third-party rights or Microsoft Store policy, including content identified in a request from Microsoft. Legal, intellectual-property or safety requests and moderation appeals can be sent to info@planetcreations.net. Further details are in the Legal Notice / Impressum.
