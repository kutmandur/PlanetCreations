Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 August 2026

PlanetCreations is a free, independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Frontier Developments plc. The service is operated by Don Reichau in Switzerland. Contact: info@planetcreations.net. Further details are in the Legal Notice / Impressum.

Information processed

Account and profile information: When you create an account, PlanetCreations processes your email address, account identifier, username, profile details, preferences and authentication status. If you connect Discord, associated Discord identifiers and profile information are processed.

Community content: Creations, descriptions, images, videos, comments, communities, events, collaborations, reports and moderation records are stored when you submit or interact with them.

Desktop client information: The client creates a random client identifier and a user-editable device display name so queued actions can reach the correct computer. Client version, platform and error details may be processed to provide or troubleshoot the service.

Notifications and personalization: Notification preferences and push tokens are stored when you enable notifications. Recommendation interests are processed only after opt-in and can be disabled in Settings.

Local files and credentials

The desktop client accesses only the game, backup and media folders needed for features you use. You choose the local Frontier Developments folder through a native folder dialog. Savegames, backups and media remain on your device unless you explicitly upload or share them. Files attached to a Creation or collaboration are transferred as signed PlanetCreations packages only after a native upload confirmation.

In online mode, the desktop client displays the PlanetCreations.net web interface. That trusted interface receives a limited desktop bridge for features such as selecting configured game files, reading an in-game preview and starting a confirmed upload. It does not receive unrestricted file-system access.

The desktop client may also load the Offline Manager interface from PlanetCreations.net so interface-only improvements can be delivered without replacing the installed application. In that view, the trusted interface receives capability-scoped access to the configured game, backup and media locations needed for the local management action you request, including scanning files, creating or restoring backups and installing or removing workshop or media packages. If the hosted interface is unavailable or incompatible, both the GitHub-distributed and Microsoft Store clients use their bundled Offline Manager instead. The hosted interface cannot add native permissions or capabilities to the installed application, does not receive general access to unrelated folders, and an upload still requires native confirmation.

OBS passwords and Streamlabs tokens are stored only on your device and encrypted through operating-system credential protection provided by Electron safeStorage. They are used solely to connect to locally configured streaming software and are not uploaded to PlanetCreations.

Purposes and legal controls

Information is used for authentication, profiles, community features, content sharing, collaborations, signed backups, Direct Install, moderation, notifications, security, fraud prevention and user-requested support. PlanetCreations does not sell personal information.

Service providers

PlanetCreations uses Google Firebase for authentication, databases, server functions, application-integrity checks and optional push notifications; Cloudflare R2 for uploaded package files; Discord for optional account linking and community integration; and Twitch or YouTube for user-selected streaming or embedded-media features. The GitHub-distributed desktop version contacts GitHub for application updates. The Microsoft Store version receives application updates only through Microsoft Store.

These providers may process technical and account information according to their own privacy notices and may process data outside your country where legally permitted.

Retention and your choices

Information is retained while required to provide the service, meet security and moderation obligations, resolve disputes or comply with law. You can edit profile data, disable personalization and notifications, unlink integrations and delete your account through Settings. Account deletion removes or anonymizes associated information where possible; limited security, moderation or legal records may be retained when required.

Security

PlanetCreations uses access controls, transport encryption, package-integrity verification and operating-system encryption for local streaming credentials. No online service can guarantee absolute security; use a unique password and keep your device protected.

Minimum age and children

You must be at least 16 years old to create an account, submit content or use account-based features. PlanetCreations does not knowingly permit account registration by anyone under 16, including with parental or guardian permission. If an underage account is identified, it may be suspended and deleted together with associated personal information, subject to limited legal, security and abuse-prevention retention. Public pages that do not require an account may still be viewed where permitted by law. Content that exploits or endangers minors is prohibited and will be removed.

Contact and changes

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing of, or receive a portable copy of personal information. Requests can be sent to info@planetcreations.net. You may also contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner or another competent supervisory authority. This policy may be updated when features or providers change; the effective date identifies the current version.
